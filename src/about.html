About

The Bombers are a men's league hockey team playing at Oaks Center Ice in Oaks, PA.

We formed in 2017 and have been playing in either their C3 and C4 divisions. We go out, (try to) skate some, have some fun, get some exercise and enjoy some beers after.

Contact the team by emailing bombershockey@kpwags.com
